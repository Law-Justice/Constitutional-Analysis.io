The Convention of 1787

Level 2 of The Committee of Five. Philadelphia, summer 1787, eleven years after the Declaration. Walk the city, watch the delegates argue out the Constitution, and follow its amendments through time with a lawyer’s eye.


Back to the three assignment options. You only need to complete one option.

Move: arrow keys or WASD. Sprint: hold Shift. Talk: E, Z, Enter, or Space. Satchel: Esc. Quotes are real and labeled by source. The map is stylized, not to scale, and the narration is the game’s own.
