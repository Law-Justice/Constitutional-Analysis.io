The Clerk’s Journal

An interactive graphic novel of the Constitution, from 1787 to today

You are a young clerk in Philadelphia in 1787. As the Constitution is written, signed, and changed, you keep a journal: you read the story, study the sources, answer questions, and put the pages together yourself.

  1. Read each comic page and turn it with Next or the arrow keys.
  2. Clip quotes, lens notes, and dated clippings to your scrapbook.
  3. Write the journal entries your teacher assigned: seven Articles and 27 amendments.
  4. Publish a front page for every era, pinning your best finds.
  5. Download your essay as a Word document and keep your finished book.

Back to the assignment options. You only need to complete one option.

Quotes are real and labeled by source. The art is stylized and the narration is the game’s own. Your work saves in this browser.
