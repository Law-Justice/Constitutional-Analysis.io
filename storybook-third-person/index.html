The Convention of 1787

Level 2 of The Committee of Five. Philadelphia, summer 1787, eleven years after the Declaration. Explore a bright storybook Philadelphia, watch the delegates argue out the Constitution, climb the trials, and follow its amendments through time with a lawyer’s eye.


Back to the three assignment options. You only need to complete one option.

Move: WASD. Jump: Space. Sprint: hold Shift. Look: just move the mouse, or use the arrow keys; the camera also follows behind you, and C swings it back. Talk: E or Enter. Satchel: Esc. Quotes are real and labeled by source. The map is stylized, not to scale, and the narration is the game’s own.
